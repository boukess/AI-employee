# Outreach sequence: Shorts Starter

Offer: Shorts Starter, $400 a month, 8 edited vertical shorts and 8 covers, 48 hour turnaround per batch (from `store/products.json`). Audience: creators and small businesses who post long video or podcasts but few or no shorts. Outside the local area only (the Lead Gen veto from batch 1 stands).

Every email is a draft. The Sales Employee saves them into your Gmail drafts; you send. Fill every `{bracket}` from something real you saw on their channel. If you cannot fill it, skip that lead.

Sending rules: your real name and BOUKE LLC, a working reply address, your postal address in the footer, and a one line opt out. Stop the sequence the moment someone replies or opts out.

---

## Steps

1. Lead Gen sources 20 creators that match the audience above and exports them.
2. For the first 5, you or I watch one video each and fill the brackets.
3. Video Editor cuts the free sample clip for each (from their public video, used only as a private pitch sample sent to them, never posted).
4. Sales Employee saves Email 1 drafts in Gmail. You review and send.
5. Chief of Staff tracks replies in its daily brief.
