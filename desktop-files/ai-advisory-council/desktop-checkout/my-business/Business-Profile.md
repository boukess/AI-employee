# Business Profile

Status: not filled in
Last updated: never

Your council reads this before every meeting. Real numbers make the advice specific. Rough numbers beat none, and "Unknown" is a fine answer.

**The fast way: let the AI interview you.** It asks 12 short questions in one message and fills this in for you.
- **Local folder:** say "Set up my council", or paste `prompts/01-SETUP.txt`. The AI saves this file.
- **Web project:** paste `prompts/01-SETUP.txt` into a chat in your council project. Save the profile it gives you as `Business-Profile.md` (or `.txt`) and upload it to the project.

**By hand:** replace each `[your answer]`. Delete every "Example:" line when you are done (they describe a made-up business). Change the Status line to `Status: filled in` and put today's date on the Last updated line.

Leave out passwords, account numbers and customers' personal details.
