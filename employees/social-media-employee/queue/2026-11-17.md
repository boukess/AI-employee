## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Stop tilting your phone at the building
**Niche:** lifestyle / small-business photography
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your storefront photo looks crooked because you tilted the phone up."
**Length:** 20-30s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Stand close to a storefront, café front, or your own front door and tilt the phone up at the sign. Show the screen: walls leaning in. Text: TILTED.
2. Quick clip of you walking backward across the sidewalk (only where it's safe and public).
3. Open camera settings and turn on the grid (and the level, if your phone has it). Screen recording or over-the-shoulder.
4. Hold the phone level at chest height, line the door frame up with a grid line. Show the screen. Text: LEVEL + GRID.
5. Side-by-side of the two real photos. Same phone, no filter.
6. Face cam line: "Back up, hold it level, use the grid. Crop the sidewalk later."
7. End card: "frbouke".

"Your storefront photo looks crooked because you tilted the phone up. That's why the walls lean in. Back up. Turn on the grid. Hold the phone level at chest height and line the door frame up with a grid line. Same phone, straight building. Crop the extra sidewalk later."
