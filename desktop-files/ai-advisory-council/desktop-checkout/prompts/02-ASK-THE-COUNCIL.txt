AI ADVISORY COUNCIL | PROMPT 02: ASK THE COUNCIL

Use for any real business decision. Works in your council project (web) or your council folder (local).
Short version: just type "Ask the council: [your question]".
Best results: a real decision with a number in it, not a general topic.
Fill in the [BRACKETS], then paste everything below the line.

================ COPY EVERYTHING BELOW THIS LINE ================

Ask the council: [YOUR QUESTION OR DECISION. Example: Should I raise my price from $249 to $299 a month, or add a cheaper tier first?]

What's new since my profile: [NEW NUMBERS OR CHANGES, OR "nothing"]
I need to decide by: [DATE, OR "no deadline"]
Seat: [ADVISOR NAMES, "you choose" OR "full council"]

Run a council meeting as set out in Council-Protocol.md.
Chair: restate my decision as a choice, pull what matters from my business profile, and seat the 3 to 5 advisors who fit (all of them if I said full council), including at least one who is likely to disagree.
Each advisor answers on their own, in their own voice, from their own brain: a diagnosis, a recommendation using one of their named frameworks with my numbers, and one thing to do this week. Keep their real disagreements. No echoing.
Then the Chair's synthesis: where they agree, where they split and the real trade-off, the call for my situation (no averaging; weigh each advisor by their blind spots), 3 next actions, what would change the call, and who to follow up with.

Local folder: follow AGENTS.md and save the meeting to sessions/.
