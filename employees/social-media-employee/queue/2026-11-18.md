## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Your sky is white because your phone is guessing
**Niche:** phone photography
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your sky came out white because your phone guessed wrong."
**Length:** 20-30s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Point the phone at a scene with bright sky and something darker in front (a tree, a car, a building, a person who agreed to be filmed). Snap without touching anything. Show the screen: blown-out white sky. Text: PHONE GUESSED.
2. Screen recording or over-the-shoulder: tap on the bright part of the sky. Text: TAP THE BRIGHT PART.
3. Drag the exposure slider (sun icon on iPhone, brightness slider on Android) down until the sky gets its color back. Text: SLIDE DOWN.
4. Long-press to lock exposure/focus (AE/AF LOCK on iPhone; tap-and-hold lock on most Androids). Snap.
5. Side-by-side of the two real photos. Same phone, no filter.
6. Face cam line: "Tap the bright part, slide it down. Shadows you can lift later, white sky you can't."
7. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your sky came out white because your phone guessed wrong. It exposed for the dark stuff. Tap the brightest part of the frame, slide the exposure down until the sky comes back, then hold to lock it. Same phone, real sky. You can lift shadows later. You can't bring back a white sky."

Use only your own photos; if a stranger or a business is in frame, ask first or keep them out of frame.
