# Council Roster

Who sits on your council, what each advisor is for, who to call for which question, and where they agree and clash. The Chair reads this before every meeting.

Every advisor is an AI built from a person's public work: books, interviews, podcasts, talks and posts. None of them is the real person, and none is affiliated with or endorsed by the person. Research is current to September 2026.

Last updated: September 2026 (five course advisors).

## Add or remove an advisor

Your council can hold more than five advisors. Seat 3 to 5 in any one meeting. Give each new advisor a seat the council does not already cover, such as operations, finance, hiring, sales or your own industry.

### Add an advisor you cloned (local folder)

1. Say **"Clone <name>'s brain."** To clone yourself, put your files in `clone-a-brain/sources/` and say **"Clone my brain."**
2. The AI follows `clone-a-brain/CLONE-PROCEDURE.md`. It writes the new advisor into `advisors/<slug>/` and adds them to this file: one row in the seats table marked "(cloned, <Month YYYY>)", their name in the routing rows that match their seat, and their agree and clash lines from section 13 of their brain.
3. Say **"Test my clone."** Then ask them: "Ask <name>: ..." or seat them with "Ask the council: ...".

If you already have a finished advisor (a Brain file and an Instructions file made elsewhere), put them in `advisors/<slug>/` and say **"Add <name> to the council."** The AI reads the brain and adds the roster entries the same way.

### Add an advisor on the web

1. Build the clone with the Clone-A-Brain Kit prompts: `clone-a-brain/prompts/01` to `03`, or `05` for yourself or a mentor who agreed. Test it with `04`.
2. Upload the new `<Name>-Brain.md` to your council project's knowledge. Check your file limit first: ChatGPT allows 5 files per project on Free, 25 on Go and Plus, 40 on Pro and up.
3. In a chat inside the council project, paste Part B of `prompts/07-CLONE-A-NEW-ADVISOR.txt`. It gives you an updated Council-Roster.md.
4. Save it, delete the old Council-Roster.md from project knowledge, and upload the new one.

You do not need to change `Council-Instructions.txt`. The Chair seats any advisor listed here whose Brain file is in the project.

### What a new advisor needs

- A Brain file built on the 13-section template in `clone-a-brain/templates/`. The Chair leans hardest on section 3 (frameworks), section 10 (blind spots) and section 13 (council behavior).
- A unique file prefix, such as `Jane-Smith`, so files never clash.
- A row in the seats table, entries in the routing guide, and agree and clash lines with the existing advisors in the map.
- For a private person: their permission, recorded in their Sources file.

### Remove or bench an advisor

- **Local folder:** say **"Remove <name> from the council"** or **"Bench <name>."** The AI moves `advisors/<slug>/` to `advisors/_benched/<slug>/` (nothing is deleted) and removes their row, routing mentions and map lines from this file. **"Bring back <name>"** reverses it.
- **Web project:** delete their Brain file from project knowledge. The Chair only seats advisors whose Brain file is there.
- **For one meeting:** say who to leave out ("Ask the council without Gary: ...") or name who to seat.

### Update an advisor

- **Local folder:** say **"Update <name>'s brain."** The AI follows Part D of `clone-a-brain/CLONE-PROCEDURE.md` and researches what is new since the brain's research date.
- **Web project:** follow "Keeping it current" in `clone-a-brain/Clone-A-Brain-Method.md`. Then delete the old Brain file from project knowledge and upload the new one, so the AI never reads two versions.
