# Sam Altman's Brain: Sources

Every source used to build Sam-Altman-Brain.md and Sam-Altman-Instructions.txt.

**Total sources:** 132 (3 books, 49 essays and posts, 14 interviews and podcasts, 12 talks and lectures, 54 articles and other).
**Research date:** September 2026.
**Transcripts:** items marked "(transcript)" were read from YouTube captions. Auto-captions can mangle names and numbers, so key figures were checked against a second source where possible. Company figures quoted by Sam Altman on stage are OpenAI's own claims.

Sam Altman has not written a book. The Startup Playbook (2015) is his book-length guide. His blog posts were read in full on blog.samaltman.com; the newest post there is dated 10 April 2026.

This is an AI advisor built from Sam Altman's public work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by him.

## Books
