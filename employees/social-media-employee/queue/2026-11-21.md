## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Your photos are foggy because of your thumb
**Niche:** lifestyle / small-business phone photography
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your camera isn't bad. Your lens has a thumbprint on it."
**Length:** 15-25s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Close-up of a phone pulled out of a pocket, thumb sliding across the back lenses. Text: EVERY TIME YOU GRAB IT.
2. Shoot a product, coffee cup or storefront sign with the smudged lens, ideally near a light or window so the haze/glow shows. Text: BEFORE.
3. Macro-ish shot of the lens with the visible smear (angle it toward a light so it shows).
4. Wipe the lens with a soft cloth or the inside of a cotton shirt. Text: 2 SECONDS.
5. Reshoot the exact same frame. Text: AFTER.
6. Side-by-side or swipe transition: hazy vs. crisp.
7. Face cam line: "Before you blame the phone, wipe the lens. Every single time."
8. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your camera isn't bad. Your lens has a thumbprint on it. Every time you grab your phone, your fingers smear the lens, and that's the foggy glow around lights and the soft, washed-out look. Here's the shot as is. Two seconds with a soft cloth. Same shot again. Before you blame the phone, wipe the lens. Every single time."

Shoot in a space you're allowed to film; show only your own products or a business that agreed to be in it.
