## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Move 3 things out of the frame
**Niche:** lifestyle / small-business photos (counter, shelf, desk, menu item)
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your product photo isn't bad. Your background is." (photo of a product with a cord, a cup and a receipt behind it)
**Length:** 15-25s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Phone screen: product shot on a cluttered counter (cables, cup, sticky notes, register in view). Text: BEFORE.
2. Hand pulls item #1 out of frame (the cord). Text: 1.
3. Hand pulls item #2 (the cup). Text: 2.
4. Hand pulls item #3 (the paper/receipt). Text: 3.
5. Quick tip on screen: check the edges of the frame, not just the middle. Text: CHECK THE EDGES.
6. Retake from the same spot, same light. Text: AFTER.
7. Swipe between before and after.
8. Face cam line: "Before you touch a filter, move three things."
9. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your product photo isn't bad. Your background is. Before you shoot, move three things out of the frame. The cord. The cup. The receipt. Then look at the edges of the screen, not just the middle, because that's where the clutter hides. Same phone, same light, cleaner shot. Before you touch a filter, move three things."

Film on your own desk or a shop counter where the owner said yes. Keep the same angle and light for before and after so the only change is the clutter.
