# AI dropshipping plan: second Shopify store

Researched 2026-11-10. Numbers below come from vendor and review blogs, not audited data, so treat them as rough.

## What the research says

- Most dropship stores do not make money. Estimates of the share that profit consistently range from about 5 to 10 percent (TrueProfit) to 10 to 20 percent in year one, rising to 20 to 35 percent for owners who put in about $1,000 and 3 to 6 months of steady work (AdsX, Ecosire).
- Generic AliExpress margins have fallen from roughly 40 to 55 percent in 2021 to 28 to 38 percent, with 15 to 45 day shipping and nothing stopping a competitor copying you (Ecommerce Times, Ecosire).
- Print on demand (POD) shipped domestically runs about 20 to 35 percent margin with 3 to 7 day shipping, and the designs are yours, so nobody can undercut the exact product.
- The stores that work start from a real customer problem or audience, then use AI to run the work. AI does not fix a weak product choice.

## The pick: a POD store built on MKSWRLD Photography

BOUKE already owns the thing generic dropshippers lack: original images. The 16 MKSWRLD portraits and future game day shots become products nobody else can sell.

| Line | Product | Supplier app |
|---|---|---|
| Prints | Framed and poster prints of MKSWRLD shots | Printful or Gelato |
| Apparel | Tees and hoodies with BOUKE and MKSWRLD marks | Printful |
| Team merch | Custom team and parent gear after a game day shoot | Printful |
| Test lane | 3 to 5 trending accessories, US warehouse only | CJ Dropshipping (US stock) |

The test lane stays small and only uses US warehouse stock, so shipping stays under about a week.

## What the two videos add

1. **Agent inbox and contract review** (video 1). The Customer Satisfaction Employee drafts every order question reply, and the Ad Copywriter checks every brand or wholesale deal against what was agreed. Drafts only; you click send.
2. **Code rendered video ads** (video 2). The Video Editor uses the `take-one`, `video-director` and `huashu-motion` skills already in `.claude/skills` to make product ads and shorts from product photos, with motion graphics, so no filming is needed per product.

## How it runs

| Job | Who | Automatic? |
|---|---|---|
| Find products and trends weekly | Lead Gen and GTM Engineer research | Yes, report only |
| Product photos and mockups | Product Photography Employee | Yes |
| Listings, titles, SEO descriptions | Shopify connector plus SEO Employee | Drafted as unpublished products; you publish |
| Order fulfillment | Printful or CJ app inside Shopify | Yes, once you turn on auto fulfill (that spends money per order, from the sale) |
| Customer emails | Customer Satisfaction Employee | Drafts; you send, until you release the channel |
| Ads and short videos | Video Editor plus Social Media Employee | Made automatically; posting and ad spend wait for you |
| Weekly profit check | Chief of Staff and BOUKE HQ | Yes |

Spending money (ads, supplier charges) and posting stay your call, as every kit requires, until you write a release in `RELEASES.md`.

## What only you can do

1. Create the new Shopify store (Shopify needs your sign up, payment and terms acceptance) or decide to add these products to the existing store `ykpvju-5y.myshopify.com`.
2. Install Printful (and CJ if you want the test lane) from the Shopify App Store.
3. Reconnect the Shopify connector for Claude so I can draft products.
4. Set a test budget and a stop rule, for example $300 over 30 days, stop if no sale by day 21.
