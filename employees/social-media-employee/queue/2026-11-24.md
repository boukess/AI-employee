## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Stop panning with your arms
**Niche:** sports (phone video from the sideline or stands)
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your game footage is shaky because you're filming with your arms." (jittery clip of a play, phone held out at arm's length)
**Length:** 15-25s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Phone held out at arm's length, following a runner or a pass: jerky, bouncing frame. Text: ARMS ONLY.
2. Second phone (or a friend) films you from the side: elbows out, arms swinging to chase the play.
3. Close-up: elbows tucked into your ribs, both hands on the phone, feet planted shoulder-width. Text: ELBOWS IN.
4. Side angle: you turn from the hips like a turret, arms locked, phone moving with your whole upper body. Text: TURN FROM THE HIPS.
5. Same kind of play filmed the new way: smooth pan. Text: AFTER.
6. Quick swipe between before and after.
7. Bonus line on screen: keep recording 2 seconds after the whistle (celebrations are the best part). Text: DON'T STOP EARLY.
8. Face cam line: "Your arms shake. Your hips don't. Turn your body, not your hands."
9. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your game footage is shaky because you're filming with your arms. Every time you chase the play, your hands wobble. Tuck your elbows into your ribs, plant your feet, and turn from the hips. Same phone, way smoother pan. And keep rolling a couple seconds after the whistle, that's where the reactions are. Your arms shake. Your hips don't."

Film at a practice, pickup game, or your own kid's game, and only show people who are OK being on camera.
