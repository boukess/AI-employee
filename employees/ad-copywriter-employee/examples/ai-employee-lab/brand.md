# Brand profile

Last updated: 2026-10-13 (worked example)

## Business
- Name: AI Employee Lab
- What it is (one sentence): A $9/month Skool community where you download ready-built AI employees (Claude Code and ChatGPT/Codex project folders that do a real job) and learn to build your own.
- Website / landing page: https://www.skool.com/aiemployeelab/about
- Country / market: United States first, plus Canada, United Kingdom, Australia

## The offer the ads sell
- Offer: Membership in AI Employee Lab. Each ad leads with one specific AI employee members can download. For this creative, that's the AI Video Editor (raw talking-head clip in, finished short out).
- Price and terms: $9/month, cancel anytime. Everything unlocked the day you join, with no upsells and no drip timers. An annual plan also exists ($54/year at last check, so confirm before using it).
- Where the click goes: the Skool About page (skool.com/aiemployeelab/about)
- Likely button: Learn more (current live ads use "See details")

## Who it's for
- Best customer: Business owners and creators who post short-form video, freelancers and agency owners, and solo operators. Most are not technical. Many have tried AI tools that didn't stick.
- What they want: Real work done for them (editing, content, lead gen, admin) by an AI they set up once and keep using. And to keep up with AI without paying for overpriced courses.
- What's in the way / what they've tried before: Editing eats hours. Chat tools forget their business and need babysitting. Tutorials assume they can code. $97/month groups lock content behind timers.
- Their words (quotes from customers, reviews, DMs):
  - "At first I wasn't sure but I thought to myself, its $9, let me see if there's at least one thing in here thats beneficial." (public Skool review)
  - "Kyle keeps delivering." (public Skool review)
  - "For $9/month, the value is incredible." (public Skool review)
  - "Most groups don't provide even a fraction of the value that this one offers." (public Skool review)
  - Bots that "always have something broken" (member intro posts)

## Why this one
- Differentiators:
  - You download a working AI employee instead of watching a course about one.
  - A new AI employee drops every week. Members vote on what gets built next.
  - Runs in Claude Code or ChatGPT/Codex, on your own computer. Plain files you own.
  - The founder runs the community, the video and the ads on the same employees members download.
  - $9/month, everything unlocked on day one, no upsells, cancel anytime.
- Proof the ads can use (true and checkable):
  - 5.0-star Skool rating, with the public reviews quoted above
  - Current library, as stated in live ads (Oct 2026): AI Video Editor, UGC AI Employee, Lead Generation Employee, captions and headline highlighter, motion graphics, intros and split screens
  - The video editor's result screen in the creative: a 65-second raw clip cut to 38.8 seconds, with 6 cuts and 13 scenes
  - Member count: shown on the Skool page. Confirm the current number before using it.
- Proof the ads can NOT use (and why):
  - Income or client results from members (not verified, and Meta restricts earnings claims)
  - Old member counts or employee counts (they change weekly)

## Voice
- Three words: direct, practical, anti-hype
- Sounds like (a line in their voice): "Raw clip in, finished short out. It's nine bucks a month, and there's a new one every week."
- Never say: leverage, seamless, empower, game-changer, passive income, guaranteed, "no-code". Say "AI employee", never "agent", "bot" or "automation" (unless contrasting with them).
- Emojis: sparingly (check-mark lists are fine; the live ads use them)
- Em dashes: no

## Research targets
- Competitors and brands to study: other AI and automation Skool communities, Claude Code and AI-agent courses, AI video-editing tools aimed at creators, low-ticket creator memberships
- Search terms to try: "skool community", "AI employee", "claude code", "AI agents", "AI video editor", "edit your videos", "short form content", "join the community", "$9/month"
- Adjacent niches with the same kind of offer: low-ticket memberships for creators and marketers, template and prompt libraries, AI tool-of-the-week newsletters with paid tiers

## Rules
- Claims we can't make / compliance notes: no earnings claims, no "replace your editor or employees" framing (the stated belief is that AI employees work for you, not replace you), and no fake scarcity (the $9 price is not presented as a countdown).
