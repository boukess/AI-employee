## 2. Agenda item 1 — Two channel concepts

### Channel A — BOUKE Lens (photography × sports)
Name options: **BOUKE Lens**, BOUKE Sideline, BOUKE Shots.
- **Positioning:** "Behind the lens at real games" — sports photography from the sideline, how the shots are made, and the athletes in them. Doubles as the portfolio for paid shoots.
- **Audience:** local athletes and parents, high-school/college sports fans, aspiring sports photographers, local businesses.
- **Pillars:** (1) Sideline POV / game-day shoots, (2) edit breakdowns (raw → final), (3) gear and settings on a budget, (4) athlete spotlights, (5) photo challenges.
- **Formats + cadence (starting target):** 3–5 Shorts/Reels/TikToks a week, 1 long-form YouTube every 1–2 weeks, carousels of best shots 2–3×/week, weekly Pinterest pin batch.
- **First 10 ideas:** 1) "I photographed a local game with a budget camera — here's every shot" 2) Raw vs. edited in 30 seconds 3) Best settings for Friday-night lights 4) Shooting a youth league from the sideline (with permission) 5) Athlete spotlight: one player, one game 6) 5 mistakes new sports photographers make 7) $0 gear vs. pro gear same game 8) How I edit a sports photo in Lightroom in 60 s 9) Game-day vlog: shoot to delivery in one night 10) "Rate my shots" community challenge.
- **Who produces:** Video Editor (shorts/long-form edits, captions), Thumbnail (re-profiled to sports photography), Ghostwriter (scripts, captions, newsletter in your voice), Social Media (calendar + drafts per platform), SEO (YouTube titles/descriptions + site articles like "sports photographer in [city]"), Product Photography (gear/merch shots).

### Channel B — BOUKE Play (gaming × lifestyle)
Name options: **BOUKE Play**, BOUKE Life, BOUKE After Hours.
- **Positioning:** "Building a business by day, gaming by night" — gameplay and setup content plus the real lifestyle of starting BOUKE with AI employees.
- **Audience:** 16–34 gamers, young entrepreneurs/creators, people curious about AI tools.
- **Pillars:** (1) gameplay highlights + challenges, (2) setup/desk tours and gear, (3) day-in-the-life building BOUKE, (4) AI tools for creators (shows the AI employees working), (5) community game nights.
- **Formats + cadence (starting target):** 1–2 streams/week (Twitch or Kick), 4–6 clips/week cut into Shorts/TikTok/Reels, 1 YouTube long-form every 1–2 weeks, 1 newsletter/week.
- **First 10 ideas:** 1) "I hired AI employees to run my channel — week 1" 2) Desk/setup tour on a budget 3) Best clips of the week 4) Day in my life starting an LLC 5) Playing ranked until I win (challenge) 6) AI made my thumbnail vs. I did 7) Gaming with subscribers night (Discord) 8) What a $X setup gets you (fill in real price) 9) How I edit gameplay clips fast 10) Monthly "what BOUKE earned and learned" (real numbers only).
- **Who produces:** Video Editor (clips, split-screen, captions), Thumbnail, Ghostwriter, Social Media, SEO, Product Photography (gear/merch).

**Council lenses:** Gary Vee-style — volume first: clip every stream and shoot into many shorts, document don't create. MrBeast-style — packaging first: decide title + thumbnail before filming; strong first 3 seconds. Hormozi-style — the channel is the lead source; every video points to one clear offer. Chesky-style — design the experience end to end (site, booking, delivery). Altman-style — use the AI employees as leverage so one person ships like a team.

- Open a separate business bank account under BOUKE LLC with your EIN; run all income/expenses through it.
- Invoice under BOUKE LLC (EIN, not SSN); take payment via Stripe, PayPal Business, Square or Shopify Payments.
- Use a simple written agreement for every client (scope, price, delivery date, usage rights, cancellation).
- **Model releases** for portraits; for minors, parent/guardian consent; get venue/league permission before shooting games and selling photos.
- Check state registration (LLC annual report) and whether your state charges sales tax on photography prints/digital goods; local business license if required.
- Keep receipts for gear, software, mileage; consider a CPA for quarterly estimated taxes.
- Platform rules: follow each platform's terms, disclose affiliate links/sponsorships (FTC), no fake reviews, no spam DMs.
