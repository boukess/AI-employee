# BOUKE Lens: week 1 shorts

Five vertical shorts, 20 to 45 seconds each. Channel concept from the 2026-11-01 all hands: behind the lens at real games. Each short ends by pointing to frbouke.com for game day coverage or editing.

Rules for every short: only your own footage and photos; get permission before featuring an identifiable athlete, and a parent's permission for a minor; no invented numbers.

How to make them: record the clips listed, drop them in `C:\Users\blood\AI Video Editor\inbox`, and ask the Video Editor to cut each one to the script below with captions. Nothing posts until you post it.

---

## 1. Raw vs. edited in 30 seconds

- **Hook (0 to 2s), on screen:** "Same photo. 30 seconds apart."
- **Shots:** the raw frame full screen, then a screen recording of your edit sped up, then a hard cut to the final.
- **Voiceover:** "This is straight out of the camera. Flat, a little dark. Crop for the action, lift the shadows, warm the skin, kill the distractions. That's the shot."
- **Close:** "Want your game shot like this? Link in bio."
- **Caption:** Raw to final in 30 seconds. Which one would you post? #sportsphotography #photoediting #gameday
- **Need from you:** one raw game photo and a screen recording of you editing it.

## 2. Settings I use under Friday night lights

- **Hook:** "Stadium lights are lying to your camera."
- **Shots:** you on the sideline, close up of the camera dial, three example frames.
- **Voiceover:** walk through the shutter speed, aperture and ISO you actually use and why. Say only your real settings.
- **Close:** "Save this for your next game."
- **Caption:** My night game settings. Save it. #sportsphotographer #fridaynightlights #camerasettings
- **Need from you:** a clip of your camera settings screen and three night game shots.

## 3. One player, one game

- **Hook:** "I followed one player for a whole game."
- **Shots:** 8 to 12 photos of the same athlete, timed to the beat.
- **Voiceover:** none, music and captions only. Captions name the moment, not stats you do not have.
- **Close:** "Parents: game day coverage is $150. frbouke.com"
- **Caption:** One player, one game, every moment. #athletespotlight #sportsphotography
- **Need from you:** the photo set, and written permission from the athlete (and a parent if under 18).

## 4. Five mistakes new sports photographers make

- **Hook:** "I made all five of these."
- **Shots:** talking head, cutting to a bad example and a fixed example for each.
- **Points:** shutter too slow, shooting from standing height, cutting off hands and feet, chasing the ball instead of faces, over editing.
- **Close:** "Which one are you guilty of?"
- **Caption:** 5 mistakes I stopped making. Number 4 changed everything. #photographytips #sportsphotography
- **Need from you:** a 60 second talking head take and before and after pairs from your own photos.

## 5. Shoot to delivery in one night

- **Hook:** "Game ends at 9. Photos delivered by midnight."
- **Shots:** quick montage: arriving, shooting, laptop, export, the delivery message (blur names).
- **Voiceover:** short, about the turnaround.
- **Close:** "Booking game day coverage at frbouke.com"
- **Caption:** Game to gallery in one night. #gamedaycontent #sportsphotographer
- **Need from you:** phone clips across one real game night.
