## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Walk closer, don't zoom
**Niche:** phone photography
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Pinch-to-zoom is the fastest way to ruin a phone photo."
**Length:** 20-30s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Over-the-shoulder: your thumb pinch-zooming the camera app on a subject across the room (a sign, a mug, a ball, a pet). Text: DIGITAL ZOOM.
2. Snap the photo. Quick punch-in on the result so the mush and noise show.
3. Close the zoom back to 1x. Feet shot: three steps forward. Text: 3 STEPS.
4. Fill the frame at 1x (or the phone's real 2x/3x lens if it has one). Snap.
5. Side-by-side of the two real photos, same punch-in crop. Same phone, no filter.
6. Voice line: "Your feet are the best zoom lens you own."
7. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Pinch-to-zoom is the fastest way to ruin a phone photo. It's not a lens, it's a crop. Back to 1x, take three steps, fill the frame. Same phone, sharper photo. Your feet are the best zoom lens you own."

Use only your own photos; if a stranger or a business is in frame, ask first.
