# Gary Vee's Brain: Sources

Every source used to build Gary-Vee-Brain.md and Gary-Vee-Instructions.txt.

**Total sources:** 116 (7 books, 51 essays and posts, 15 interviews and podcasts, 17 talks and videos from his own channel, 26 articles and other).
**Research date:** September 2026.
**Transcripts:** items marked "(transcript)" were read from YouTube auto-captions or published podcast transcripts. Auto-captions can mangle names and numbers, so key figures were checked against a second source where possible. Book content was read through Gary's own excerpts and pages or through reader notes, and is paraphrased in the brain.

This is an AI advisor built from Gary Vaynerchuk's public work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by him.

## Books

1. **Crush It!** (2009). Read via Marlo Yonocruz's book notes: https://marloyonocruz.com/2020/08/20/book-notes-crush-it-by-gary-vaynerchuk/. The 2009 baseline: one passion, content as lure, delay monetization, "work at night" framing.
2. **The Thank You Economy** (2011). Read via Gary's book excerpt in Entrepreneur, 16 Mar 2011: https://www.entrepreneur.com/leadership/building-a-business-in-the-thank-you-economy/219296. Small-town shop rules at scale, not ranking customers by visible spend, answering complaints like a dinner guest.
3. **Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook** (2013). Read via chapter notes at marketingfirst.co.nz, Jul 2014: https://www.marketingfirst.co.nz/2014/07/jab-jab-jab-right-hook-how-to-tell-your-story-in-a-noisy-social-world-by-gary-vaynerchuk/. Jabs and hooks, "Content is king, but context is God," native content rules.
4. **#AskGaryVee** (Mar 2016). Read via Paul Minors' summary with book quotes: https://paulminors.com/blog/askgaryvee-book-summary-pdf/. First customers ("You can't be shy"), a year of savings before quitting, Plan A and Plan B, earning a personal brand.

6. **Twelve and a Half** (2021). Read via Gary's post "Twelve and a Half: A Taste of the Ingredients," 12 Jan 2022: https://garyvaynerchuk.com/twelve-and-a-half-a-taste-of-the-ingredients/. The 12 emotional ingredients plus kind candor.
