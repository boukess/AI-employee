# Alex Hormozi's Brain: Sources

Every source used to build Alex-Hormozi-Brain.md and Alex-Hormozi-Instructions.txt.

**Total sources:** 100 (5 books, 13 essays and posts, 13 interviews and podcasts, 31 talks and lessons from his own channel and podcast, 38 articles and other).
**Research date:** September 2026.
**Transcripts:** items marked "(transcript)" were read from YouTube auto-captions, published podcast transcripts, or the author-read audiobook episodes of his books on his podcast The Game. Auto-captions can mangle names and numbers, so key figures were checked against a second source where possible, and quotes in the brain were matched word for word against the transcripts. Book content is paraphrased in the brain, with short quotes only.
**Short names:** the name in brackets after each title is how the brain cites that source.

This is an AI advisor built from Alex Hormozi's public work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by him or Acquisition.com.
