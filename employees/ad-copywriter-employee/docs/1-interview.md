# Step 1: Interview

Goal: learn enough about the business to research the right ads and write copy that sounds like the member. Be quick about it. Most members should be done in 3 to 5 messages.

## How to run it

- Ask in rounds of two or three questions. Use the order below, and skip anything you already know.
- If the member pastes a website, landing page or social profile, read it first (WebFetch in Claude Code, web search or browsing in Codex and ChatGPT). Pre-fill everything you can. Then ask only for what's missing, and show what you found so they can correct it.
- Accept short answers. "Not sure" is a fine answer to the nice-to-have questions.
- If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up at most ("Who buys it most often?"). Then move on.
- If they give you a lot at once (a pasted doc or a long voice note), pull out the answers and only ask about the gaps.
- Never make up an answer. Leave a field as "unknown" rather than guess.

## The questions

**Round 1: the basics**
1. What's the business (or personal brand) called, and what do you sell? Paste a website or landing page if you have one.

**Round 2: the offer**
2. What exactly will the ads sell? (the product, a free lead magnet, a call, a trial, a community...) What does it cost, and where does the click go?
3. Who's it for? Describe your best customer in a sentence or two: who they are, what they want, and what's in their way.

**Round 3: why you**
4. Why do people pick you over the alternatives? What do customers say after they buy?
5. What proof can the ads use? Real numbers, reviews, results, years in business, customer counts, press. Only what's true and you're comfortable printing.

**Round 4: voice and limits**
6. How should the brand sound? Three words, or paste a post, email or caption you wrote that sounds like you.
7. Anything the ads must never say? Claims you can't make, words you hate, or industry rules (health, finance, legal...).

**Round 5: research targets**
8. Which competitors, or brands you admire, run ads? Names or Facebook pages. "Not sure" is fine.
9. Which country are the ads running in? (default: United States)

## Done when

You must have:

- [ ] Business name and what it sells
- [ ] The specific offer the ads push (what, price or "free", and terms)
- [ ] Where the click goes, plus a likely button (Learn more, Sign up, Shop now...)
- [ ] Who it's for, plus at least one core desire or pain
- [ ] Voice: three words or a writing sample
- [ ] Country

Nice to have (don't block on these): proof, differentiators, competitors, banned claims, the customer's own words.

When the must-haves are in, stop asking. Write the brand file, show the summary, and start research in the same turn.

## The summary you show

Keep it to about five lines, then move on:

> Got it. Here's what I'll work from:
> - **[Name]**: [one-sentence offer, price]
> - **For**: [audience] who want [desire]
> - **Proof**: [best 1 or 2 proof points]
> - **Voice**: [three words]
>
> Tell me if anything's off. Next I'm going to study the ads already running in your space.

Then go straight into Step 2 (`docs/2-research.md`).
