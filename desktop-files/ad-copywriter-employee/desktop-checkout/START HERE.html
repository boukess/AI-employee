AI Ad Copywriter

An AI employee that writes your Facebook and Instagram ad copy. Give it your creative, get back three variations of primary text, headline and description, each based on real ads already running in your niche.

How it works

  1. It interviews youYour business, your offer, who it's for, and how you sound. About 3 minutes.
  2. It studies your nicheIt pulls the ads your competitors are running from the Meta Ad Library and works out which ones have run long enough to be working. About 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. It writes your copyDrop in an image or video ad. Get three checked, ready-to-paste variations with different angles. About 2 minutes per creative.

You only do the first two steps once. After that: drop a creative, get copy.

Set it up

Pick the tool you use.

  1. Unzip the download and move the AI Ad Copywriter folder to your Desktop.
  2. Open the folder in Claude Code. In the Claude desktop app: Code tab, then "Open folder". Or in Terminal: 
    cd ~/Desktop/"AI Ad Copywriter"
claude
  3. Type this and press Enter: 
    go
  4. When it asks to run a script, choose Yes, and don't ask again. The scripts only touch files inside this folder.

To give it a creative, drag the image or video into the Claude Code window. If you have the Claude in Chrome extension, Claude can open the Ad Library itself. Otherwise it gives you links to paste back in.

Copying ads from the Ad Library

In the research step, the copywriter may ask you to grab a few pages. For each link it gives you:

  1. Open the linkIt opens a Meta Ad Library search. No login needed.
  2. Scroll down 3 or 4 timesMore ads load as you scroll.
  3. Select all and copyCmd+A then Cmd+C on Mac. Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C on Windows.
  4. Paste it into the chatIt's fine if it looks messy. It only needs the text.

Want to see an example first?

Open the examples/ai-employee-lab folder. It shows two steps for a real business: the brand file the interview produces, and how the copywriter reads a 47-second video ad (frames, transcript and creative read). Or type show me the example after you start.

Good to know

  • Your business info stays in the workspace folder on your computer (in ChatGPT, it stays in your chat and the workspace zip).
  • It never makes up numbers, reviews or results. If it needs a fact, it asks you.
  • Every variation is checked against Meta's length limits and ad policies before you see it.
