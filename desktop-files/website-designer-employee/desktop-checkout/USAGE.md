# Using the kit

AI Employee Lab members may use and adapt the original employee instructions, scene code, and supporting templates to build their own or client websites. Replace the fictional example content with accurate, authorized business material. Keep third-party notices with the code they cover.

The generic cinematic starter excludes Kyle's portrait and the Lab's business identity. The separate AI Employee Lab source project is a worked brand example: replace its name, portrait, logo, copy, and links before using it for another business.

Original SCENE scenes were authored for this kit. No GetLayers proprietary code, prompts, paid templates, or artwork is included. Reference websites can inform broad techniques; do not carry over their distinctive copy, logos, brand assets, or composition as a substitute for your own direction.
