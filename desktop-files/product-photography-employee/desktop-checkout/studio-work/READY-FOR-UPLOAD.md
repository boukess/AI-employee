# Ready for upload — product photo checklist

Last updated: 2026-10-28 (America/New_York)

Use this before the first studio shoot. Generation stays idle until a real source photo lands in chat or `studio-work/sources/`.

## Photo requirements
- **Whole item** in frame (not a tight crop that hides shape or edges)
- **In focus** — product sharp; soft background is fine
- **Label / marks readable** when the product has packaging, print, or a logo
- One clear hero angle is enough to start; add a second view only if a defining face is hidden
- Prefer even light (window or soft indoor); avoid heavy glare on glossy labels
- Phone photos are fine

## What not to send as the only source
- Mood boards or competitor shots (those are style refs, not the product edit target)
- Logo files alone with no product
- Screenshots of ads that invent packaging we do not have

## After upload
1. Source is copied under `studio-work/sources/` — **never overwrite** originals
2. One square studio hero (default: warm cream backdrop, soft light, no props)
3. Output goes to `studio-work/outputs/` after fidelity check
