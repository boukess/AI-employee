## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Stop shooting from standing height
**Niche:** phone photography (works for sports, pets, products, people)
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your photos look boring because you take them from here." (phone held at chest height)
**Length:** 15-25s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Face cam, phone held at chest height pointed down at a subject (shoes, a ball, a dog, a coffee cup on a table). Text: WHERE EVERYONE SHOOTS.
2. The resulting photo from chest height: flat, top-down, cluttered floor. Text: BEFORE.
3. B-roll: you crouch or put the phone almost on the ground, phone flipped upside down so the lens sits lower. Text: GET LOW.
4. Same subject shot from the low angle: background becomes sky/wall, subject looks bigger. Text: AFTER.
5. Quick swipe between before and after, two more pairs if you have them (sneakers, basketball on court, controller on desk).
6. Face cam line: "Drop to their level, or lower. Same phone, different photo."
7. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your photos look boring because you take them from here. Chest height, pointing down, the same angle as every other photo. Get low. Flip the phone upside down so the lens is near the ground. Now the background is cleaner and your subject looks bigger. Same phone, same subject, different photo. Drop to their level, or lower."

Film only your own stuff, or people and places that agreed to be in it.
