## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** Stop using 0.5x for the family photo
**Niche:** phone photography / lifestyle (Thanksgiving table)
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your uncle's head isn't that wide. It's the 0.5x lens." (group photo on ultra-wide, person on the edge stretched)
**Length:** 15-25s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Phone screen: group at the table shot on 0.5x from close up. Text: 0.5x.
2. Zoom in on the edge of the frame: the person on the end looks stretched. Text: EDGES STRETCH.
3. Finger taps 1x on the camera app. Text: TAP 1x.
4. You take two or three steps back to fit everyone in. Text: STEP BACK.
5. Quick tip on screen: put the people you care about most in the middle, not on the edges.
6. Retake on 1x from the new spot, same light. Text: AFTER.
7. Swipe between before and after.
8. Face cam line: "Save 0.5x for the room. Use 1x for the people."
9. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your uncle's head isn't that wide. It's the 0.5x lens. Ultra-wide stretches whatever is near the edges of the frame, and at a family table that's people's faces. Tap 1x, take a few steps back, and fit everyone in from there. Keep the people closest to the edges a little further in. Same phone, same table, nobody looks stretched. Save 0.5x for the room. Use 1x for the people."

Film at your own Thanksgiving table and only post family members who said yes. Keep the same light for before and after so the only change is the lens and distance.
