SOURCES: YOUR OWN FILES

Put your own material here when you want to clone yourself, or a mentor who has said yes. Then open this kit in Claude Code or Codex and say "Clone my brain".

Best material, in order:
1. Transcripts of you talking: coaching calls, podcasts, YouTube videos, Looms, voice notes where you explain a decision.
2. Your SOPs, playbooks, training and onboarding docs.
3. Your posts, newsletters and emails to your team or customers.
4. Answers you have given to common customer or team questions.

Aim for at least 3 hours of you talking, or 20,000 words of writing.

Accepted: .txt, .md, .pdf, .docx, .srt, .vtt, and a links.txt file with one YouTube or web link per line. Transcripts work better than audio or video files.

Before you add anything, remove client names, contact details, passwords, account numbers and anything you would not want your whole team to read.

Using ChatGPT or Claude in the browser instead? Use prompts/05-Clone-From-Your-Own-Files.txt.
