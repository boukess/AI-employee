## Top short-form idea (film today)
**Title:** One window beats a ring light
**Niche:** lifestyle / small-business photography
**Hook (first 1-2s):** "Your food photo looks bad because of the ceiling, not your phone."
**Length:** 20-30s vertical 9:16

### Shot list
1. Phone shot of a plate, coffee, or product under overhead lights (kitchen or a café table). Text: CEILING LIGHT. Flat, yellow, harsh shadows.
2. Pick it up and walk it to the nearest window (fast cut, feet + hands).
3. Set it down with the window to the side, not behind you. Text: SIDE WINDOW.
4. Hold a white sheet of paper or a menu on the opposite side to bounce light. Text: + PAPER.
5. Side-by-side of the two real stills (before / after). Same phone, no filter.
6. Voice line: "Same phone. Same plate. Different light."
7. End card: "frbouke".

### Script
"Your food photo looks bad because of the ceiling, not your phone. Pick it up. Walk it to a window. Light from the side, white paper on the other side. Same phone, same plate, different light. That's the whole trick."

Use only your own stills; if you film inside a business, ask the owner first.
