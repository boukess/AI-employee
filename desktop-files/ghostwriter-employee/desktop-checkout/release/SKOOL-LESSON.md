# AI Ghostwriter Employee

Turn your ideas into polished emails, articles, and stories that sound like you.

This employee helps find samples of how you already write and speak, builds your voice notes, and turns rough ideas into complete drafts. It also suggests topics when you are not sure what to write.

## Get started

1. Download **AI Ghostwriter Employee.zip** attached to this lesson.
2. Add it to a Claude or ChatGPT conversation.
3. Send **Go. Read the AI Ghostwriter Employee instructions and start.**

If your chat cannot open the zip, unzip it and upload **GHOSTWRITER.txt** from inside the folder. Then say **Go**. The text file contains the full workflow. You do not need to upload the other files or run any code.

Your employee starts with the context and sources it can access. It might ask for your name or one profile link. If there is nothing to find online, it will help you use a few sent emails, posts, video transcripts, or a quick voice-dictated answer.

## Try it

- “Turn this idea into an email to my customers.”
- “Write an article explaining this in my voice.”
- “Help me tell the story of what happened.”
- “Give me three topics I could write about this week.”

When a sentence does not sound like you, rewrite that one line and say **Learn from this correction**. Those edits make the profile more useful.

## Keep your progress

Say **Save my progress** and keep the memory file or text it gives you. In a new conversation, add **GHOSTWRITER.txt** and **MY-GHOSTWRITER-MEMORY.txt**, then say **Continue**.

The employee uses the tools your assistant actually has. The download itself does not unlock your old conversations or computer. If it cannot access a source, it will give you a simple alternative. It works during your active session.
