# Brian Chesky's Brain: Sources

Every source used to build Brian-Chesky-Brain.md and Brian-Chesky-Instructions.txt.

**Total sources:** 78 (1 book, 18 essays and pieces of writing, 21 interviews and podcasts, 12 talks and keynotes, 26 articles and other).
**Research date:** September 2026.
**Transcripts:** items marked "(transcript)" were read from YouTube auto-captions, a published podcast transcript, or (for Invest Like the Best) a local transcription of the episode audio. Auto-captions can mangle names and numbers, so key figures were checked against Airbnb's SEC filings or a second source where possible. Numbers Chesky gives on stage are his own claims and are labeled that way in the brain.

This is an AI advisor built from Brian Chesky's public work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by him or by Airbnb.

## Books

Brian Chesky has not written a book. One book about him and Airbnb was used.

1. **Leigh Gallagher, The Airbnb Story** (2017). Read via the official excerpt "Airbnb's Surprising Path to Y Combinator," Wired, Feb 2017: https://www.wired.com/2017/02/airbnbs-surprising-path-to-y-combinator/. The cereal-box story in detail (1,000 hand-glued boxes, $40 each, mailed to reporters), Y Combinator terms, Paul Graham's "cockroaches," Michael Seibel's push to apply.

## Essays and writing

2. **"Don't Fuck Up the Culture"** (letter to all staff, sent 21 Oct 2013; published on Medium 2014). https://medium.com/@bchesky/dont-fuck-up-the-culture-597cde9ee9d4. Peter Thiel's advice, culture as the machine that makes the product, culture replacing process.
3. **"A Great New Year"** (Medium photo-essay, 1 Jan 2014). https://medium.com/@bchesky/a-great-new-year-650331381a41. His photo-essay format: real hosts, one line each, building to a thesis.
4. **"The $21 Million Problem"** (Medium, 18 Apr 2014). https://medium.com/@bchesky/the-21-million-problem-cd9fef47bf9f. Asking New York to let Airbnb collect taxes instead of seeking tax breaks.
5. **"Belong Anywhere"** (Medium, 16 Jul 2014). https://medium.com/@bchesky/belong-anywhere-ccf42702d010. The 2014 rebrand method: discover the mission in what customers already feel; the Sebastian and Amol stories.
6. **"Shared City"** (Medium photo-essay, 2014). https://medium.com/@bchesky/shared-city-db9746750a3a. Commitments offered to cities before being asked (taxes, safety kits, local causes).
7. **"7 Rejections"** (Medium, 12 Jul 2015). https://medium.com/@bchesky/7-rejections-7d894cbaa084. The June 2008 raise ($150,000 at $1.5M), five rejections and two non-replies, and why he published them.

9. **"How to Time Travel"** (Medium, 24 Dec 2015). https://medium.com/@bchesky/how-to-time-travel-b604096d5ed0. "Repetition doesn't create memories. New experiences do."
10. **"Don't Edit Your Imagination"** (Medium, Sunday Night Series, 5 Jan 2016). https://medium.com/@bchesky/don-t-edit-your-imagination-e51969c37d1b. Imagine first, edit second; the written root of the 11-star exercise.
