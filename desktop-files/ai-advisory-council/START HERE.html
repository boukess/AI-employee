3 Ask your first question

Bring a real decision you are stuck on, with a number in it. Not a general topic.

Ask the council: Should I raise my price from $249 to $299 a month, or add a cheaper tier first?

You get the decision restated as a choice, the facts from your profile that matter, and an answer from each seated advisor with one thing to do this week. Then the Chair's call, your next 3 actions and what would change the call.

Six meeting formats

Just type the words in quotes. Copy-paste versions are in the prompts below.

Council meeting

"Ask the council: ..."

The Chair seats 3 to 5 advisors. Each gives a diagnosis, a recommendation with a named framework, and one thing to do this week. Then the Chair makes the call. Say "Full council: ..." for all five.

Best for any real decision.

Ask one advisor

"Ask Hormozi: ..."

One advisor in their own voice. They may ask you up to 3 questions first, then end with "Do this week". Gary, Sam, Chesky and MrBeast work too.

Best when you know whose lane it is.

Debate

"Debate Hormozi vs Chesky: ..."

Two advisors argue their real positions over three rounds: opening, rebuttal and closing. The Chair gives a verdict for your business.

Best when two paths pull you different ways.

Hot seat

"Hot seat: ..."

The advisors ask you up to 12 sharp questions first. Answer by number, or say "go", and the full meeting runs with what they learned.

Best for big decisions.

Board review

"Board review"

Once a month, every advisor. Your numbers go in. A scorecard, each advisor's read and 3 priorities with owners and dates come out.

Best for staying on track.

Quick poll

"Quick poll: ..."

One line from each advisor: yes, no, not yet or depends. The Chair tallies the split and says what it turns on.

Best for small calls and gut checks.

You can also say

  • "Update my profile: ..." when something changes in your business.
  • "Follow up with Chesky" or "Go deeper with Sam" to continue the last meeting with one advisor.
  • "Ask the council without Gary: ..." to leave someone out of one meeting.
  • Local folder only: "Show my past meetings" and "Log outcome: ...". Every meeting is saved in sessions/.

4 What a meeting looks like

A short example of what comes back when you ask the council a question.

Example written by the AI

For illustration only. This was written by the AI for a made-up business, and every number is made up too. Each answer is shortened. Your meetings use your own profile and run longer.

Council meeting: raise the membership price

28 September 2026 | Seated: Alex Hormozi, Sam Altman, Brian Chesky

The decision

Raise the membership from $249 to $299 a month now, or add a cheaper $149 tier first?

What matters from your profile

  • Online fitness coaching group: 120 members at $249 a month, about $29,900 a month.
  • Gross margin about 45%, so delivery costs about $137 per member a month.
  • About 6% of members cancel each month.
  • 30 leads a month, and 25% of them buy.
  • Unknown: why members cancel. Assuming it is not the price.

Seated

  • Alex Hormozi: price and unit economics are his lane.
  • Sam Altman: checks whether members love it enough to pay more.
  • Brian Chesky: the likely dissent. He would fix the experience before touching the price.
  • Not seated: Gary Vee and MrBeast. This is not a content or attention question.

AHAlex Hormozi

Diagnosis: You don't have a lead problem. You have a margin problem. At 45% you keep about $112 a member each month, and that's thin for coaching.

Recommendation (LTGP to CAC): With 6% leaving each month, a member is worth about $1,870 in gross profit. At $299 with the same delivery cost, that's about $2,700. Same leads, same close rate, about 45% more profit per member. Add a cheaper tier only if your best-fit buyers are the ones saying no on price. So far nobody has told you that.

Do this week: Monday, set the checkout to $299 for new members. After 5 sales, check the close rate. If it holds, raise another 20% and test again.

SASam Altman

Diagnosis: Pricing is not where my thinking goes deepest, so I'll check what sits underneath it. Do members love this enough to pay more? Losing 6% a month says maybe not yet.

Recommendation (Love Before Growth): Run the "really bummed" test. Call 10 members and ask what they like, what they don't, and whether they would be really bummed if you shut down tomorrow. If 7 or more say yes and some have already sent you a friend, you have room to charge more. If fewer than 5 do, fix the product first. A higher price won't hide it.

Do this week: Book ten 15-minute calls by Thursday. Write the answers down word for word.

BCBrian Chesky

Diagnosis: I'm reasoning from principles here, because price isn't my lane. What I see: 6% leave every month. That's a five-star experience. It works, and nobody talks about it.

Recommendation (The 11-Star Experience): Pick one moment: a new member's first week. Five stars is "the login works". Seven stars is a plan built for them on day one, a check-in call on day three, and a result they can see by day seven. Deliver the seven-star week by hand to your next 10 members. Raise the price once people start telling friends, not before.

Do this week: By Friday, write the 1 to 11 star ladder for week one and circle the version you can deliver by hand.

Chair's synthesis

Where they agree: The price can rise if members feel the value. Nobody backs the cheaper tier yet.

Where they split: Hormozi says raise it now for new members. Chesky says fix the first week first. The real trade-off: about $50 more a month from every new member starting now, against charging more for an experience that still loses 6% of members a month.

The call: Raise the price to $299 for new members from Monday, and keep current members at $249 for now. Pricing is Hormozi's lane, and a new-member price can be reversed in a day. Move current members once 7 of 10 calls come back "really bummed" and monthly cancellations fall below 5%.

How I weighed them: Hormozi carries the most weight on price. Altman and Chesky are both thin on pricing by their own blind spots, so I used them for the love and retention check, not the number.

Next 3 actions:

  1. Set the checkout to $299 for new members by Monday.
  2. Call 10 members by Thursday with the "really bummed" questions.
  3. Deliver the seven-star first week to the next 10 new members within 30 days.

What would change the call: The close rate drops below 18% over the next 10 sales at $299, or fewer than 5 of 10 members say they would be really bummed.

Follow up with: Chesky, on the first week. Say: Ask Chesky: "Run the 11-star exercise on a new member's first week."

AI advisors built from public work, not the real people. Check anything important before you act.

End of the example written by the AI.

7 What you need to know

AI advisors, not the real people

Each advisor is an AI built from public work: books, interviews, podcasts, talks and posts. They are not the real people. This package is not affiliated with or endorsed by Alex Hormozi, Gary Vaynerchuk, Sam Altman, Brian Chesky, Jimmy Donaldson (MrBeast) or their companies.

Research date: September 2026

Each advisor was built from 78 to 132 sources, listed in their *-Sources.md file. Anything after September 2026 is not in their brain. In a local folder, say "Update Gary Vee's brain" to research what is new.

Advice, not guarantees

Check anything important before you act. For legal, tax, medical or investment questions, see a qualified professional.

Which plan you need

  • Claude Projects: every plan. Free has no RAG, so check the capacity indicator.
  • ChatGPT: Go or Plus for the full council. Free allows 5 files per project.
  • Local: Claude Code (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) or Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app.
  • Gemini works too: make a New Gem, paste the council instructions and add the 8 files under Knowledge.

Your data

Your profile and meetings live in this folder (local) or in your project (web). Leave out passwords, account numbers and customers' personal details.

Keeping it current

  • Your profile: say "Update my profile: ...", or run a "Board review" each month.
  • An advisor on the web: follow "Keeping it current" in clone-a-brain/Clone-A-Brain-Method.md.
  • Replacing a file in a project? Delete the old one first, so the AI never reads two versions.

If a file is rejected

If a platform rejects a .md file, rename it to .txt (for example Alex-Hormozi-Brain.txt) and upload it again. Nothing inside changes.
