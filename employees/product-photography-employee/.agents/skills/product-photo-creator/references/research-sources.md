# Photography reference corpus

Reviewed 2026-10-13. This is a curated text knowledge base: concise original notes and source links, not a copied image collection or a trained model. Linked photographs remain with their creators. No third-party reference images are bundled. These sources inform principles; the package's prompts, routes, and quality gates are original applications and are not endorsed by the publishers.

## Photography sources

### P1 — Shopify: Taking product photographs

[Original guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-photography)

A plain background and a repeatable camera setup help a catalog focus attention on the item and stay consistent. A white bounce card can open deep shadows. These ideas inform the clean-studio default and the consistency notes for later products. They do not establish universal marketplace image rules.

### P2 — Shopify: Clothing Photography: How To Photograph Clothes

[Original guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/clothing-photography)

The guide distinguishes garment presentation choices and emphasizes preparation, shot planning, useful angles, and details. This informs the apparel route: choose presentation deliberately, preserve garment construction, and do not substitute an attractive but inaccurate silhouette. Our default of retaining the source presentation is an AI-fidelity adaptation rather than a rule from the article.

### P3 — Nikon / Donna Crous: Create Your Light: Food Photography at Home

[Original guide](https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/tips-and-techniques/create-your-light-food-photography-at-home)

Directional side light gives food dimensional texture; transmitted or back light can suit clear drinks and some glossy foods. Restrained styling keeps attention on the food. These principles inform the food and drink routes. The package's requirement to preserve the actual portion and ingredients addresses generative-image fidelity.

### P4 — broncolor: How to Photoshoot Watches

[Original lighting breakdown](https://broncolor.swiss/news/how-to-photoshoot-watches-1)

The shoot separately controls edge definition, dial visibility, and small highlights. Reflective faces need light placement that avoids obscuring the important surface. This informs the jewelry route and the idea of lighting a mixed-material product around its most sensitive feature rather than applying one generic “studio lighting” phrase.

### P5 — broncolor: The ultimate guide to photograph luxury watches

[Original lighting breakdown](https://broncolor.swiss/news/the-ultimate-guide-to-photograph-luxury-watches)

Reflected gradients and local contrast describe polished surfaces. The photographer also uses focus stacking for a detailed view. These observations inform controlled reflections and deliberate sharpness. The employee requests their visible effects; it does not pretend that a generated frame was physically focus-stacked or photographed with the cited equipment.

## How to use this corpus

Start with the product's observed material, geometry, and purpose. Select a route, then a specific lighting effect, composition, and preservation list. Resolve conflicts in favor of the user's explicit brief and product identity. Use the sources for photographic principles; a distinctive campaign image is not a template to copy wholesale.

Supplement packages, cosmetics, electronics, furniture, and unfamiliar goods use the same material and geometry approach. Their category instructions are our design choices, not claims that each source provides specialist guidance for every category. No minimum camera, paid course, lighting kit, or vendor service is required to use this employee.
