# MrBeast: Sources

> Every source used to build MrBeast-Brain.md, an AI advisor built from MrBeast's (Jimmy Donaldson's) public work. It is not MrBeast, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by him.

**Researched:** September 2026
**Total sources:** 101 (1 book, 6 essays and writing, 14 interviews and podcasts, 12 from his own channels and talks, 68 articles and other), of which 36 were read from transcripts
**Research notes:** kept in the research folder of the build (not for upload)

How to read this list:
- Each source has its title, date, link, and one line on what it added to the brain.
- "Cited as" gives the short name used in the brain's brackets, for example (Handbook, p.14) or (Lex Fridman, Jan 2023).
- "(transcript)" means it was read from a full transcript (YouTube auto-captions), so quotes from it were checked word for word. Auto-captions can mangle names and numbers, so key figures were checked against a second source where possible.
- "(self-reported)" marks numbers he gave about himself that no other source confirms.
- Sources marked "council relations" were read by the researchers for the other four advisors and are used only in section 13.
