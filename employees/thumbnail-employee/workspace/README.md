# Your saved workspace

The employee stores your personal work here when file access is available:

```text
profile.json                 Creator, audience, preferences, evidence
state.json                   Progress, coverage target, next action
assets/registry.json         Asset roles and source provenance
assets/portraits/            Your optional likeness references
assets/brand/                Logos, UI screenshots, object references
corpus/competitors.jsonl     Relevant creators and source notes
corpus/candidates.jsonl      Discovered items not yet verified
corpus/references.jsonl      Indexed thumbnail observations
corpus/images/               Saved research images
corpus/patterns.md           Findings tied to reference IDs
corpus/research-log.md       Search coverage and resumable batches
corpus/gallery.html          Optional browsable contact sheet
jobs/<job-id>/               Brief, shortlist, prompt, images, QA
SESSION-CHECKPOINT.md        Portable resume instructions
```

This community download starts clean. The employee creates files when they are
needed. Your personal workspace may contain your face, raw transcripts, and other
private material; keep it for your own use rather than replacing the community ZIP.
